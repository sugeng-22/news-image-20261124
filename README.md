# The World in One Day — Tuesday 24 November 2026

A single-image news infographic for the make.fun **News Image** bounty
(news-image-20261124). `main.py` copies the bundled `entry.png` to
`/output/entry.png`; `generate.py` is the Pillow script that rendered the
image from scratch (deterministic, no network) from the bundled hero
illustration `hero.png`.

Run it with: `python3 main.py` — the image appears at `/output/entry.png`.
Re-render it with: `python3 generate.py`.

## What's in the image

- **Riyadh — Middle East:** Houthis strike Riyadh's tallest tower, the
  second attack on the tower in five days; 11 people injured.
- **Tallinn — Europe:** Russia warns Estonia of "open war" over its plan
  to close border crossings on 1 December.
- **Sport — Africa:** Senegal crowned AFCON champions; the top court
  reinstates Senegal as winners of the 2025 Africa Cup of Nations.
- **Tech — Markets:** Nvidia chief Jensen Huang returns, reinstated as CEO
  under two weeks after resigning; shares jump.
- **Space — Defence:** NATO vows to defend space; chief Mark Rutte answers
  reports of a Russian nuclear-armed satellite.
- **Seoul — AI:** South Korea pays to catch AI cheats — rewards up to
  $375,000 for tips on AI cheating in the national college exam.
- **Also today:** Israeli strikes kill a Hamas security chief in Gaza;
  Russian attack on Zaporizhzhia kills 2, wounds 40; Spain's top court
  amnesties Catalan leader Puigdemont; S&P 500 slips 0.95%.
